Sobre mí

Hey there! I’m Javier, a Senior Lighting TD, Compositor and Look dev artist with 12 years of experience in the Animation and VFX industry.

I’ve worked across feature films, TV series and commercials at studios like Bwater Animation, Red Animation Studio, Atlantis Animation, Axis Animation, Anima Kitchen, People Moving Pixel and 3doubles Productions. I also enjoy teaching, and I’ve shared my knowledge at U-TAD, Animum School of Digital Arts and 3doubles Campus.

I’m passionate about building solid, efficient pipelines, and I’m always looking for new challenges where I can combine the technical and the artistic side of production.
